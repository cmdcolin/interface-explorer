## VHL and elongin C

{VHL.counts.pathogenic} pathogenic variants of the von Hippel–Lindau tumour
suppressor sit on the {VHL.interfaceResidues} residues that hold elongin C, with
{VHL.counts.uncertain} uncertain ones among them. The crystal structure
{ELOC.experiment.pdb.toUpperCase()} puts {ELOC.experiment.experimental} residues
of VHL against elongin C, and the prediction has {ELOC.experiment.shared} of
them.

<Html html={geneCase('VHL')} />

Not every hotspot is a contact. ClinVar classifies R167G, L, P, Q and W as
pathogenic, yet in the prediction R167 sits just outside
the interface, one residue past the stretch UniProt annotates as "Interaction
with Elongin BC complex" (157–166). A 5 Å cut-off counts only the residues that
touch the partner, not the ones that hold them in place. Hover 167 on VHL's
protein map to find it in 3D.

## GNAO1, Gβ and the RGS proteins

Gαo's {GNAO1.counts.pathogenic} pathogenic interface variants fall in two
patches: the N-terminal helix that binds Gβ (L13P, I19M, L23F) and the switch
regions that bind RGS proteins (T182I, Q205P, and R206G, L and P).

## APP695, the neuronal isoform of APP

Splicing changes interfaces too. APP695 (P05067-4) has no Kunitz inhibitor
domain, so it lacks most of APP's predicted interfaces with the proteases KLK11,
KLK14 and prostasin; APP751 (P05067-8) keeps the domain and lacks none. Across
{isoforms.genes.toLocaleString()} disease genes and
{isoforms.alternativeIsoforms.toLocaleString()} modelled isoforms,
{isoforms.lackingInterface.toLocaleString()} isoforms lack half or more of a
partner interface, and {isoforms.genesLackingPathogenic.toLocaleString()} genes
have an isoform missing a residue with a pathogenic variant.
