We mapped every confident AlphaFold DB predicted complex of a human disease
protein back to the genome, codon by codon, and asked which ClinVar missense
variants land on an interface. Within the same protein, the odds of landing on
one are **{odds(e.all).toFixed(1)}×** as high for a pathogenic variant as for a
benign one.

## The effect holds on the folded surface

Interfaces lie in folded regions, where pathogenic variants concentrate anyway.
Restricting the comparison to folded residues, then to folded residues on the
surface, leaves the effect standing: among exposed residues the odds ratio is
{odds(e.orderedSurface).toFixed(1)}×.

### Within AlphaMissense's evidence bands

We repeated the comparison within each of AlphaMissense's evidence bands,
calibrated for clinical use by
[Bergquist et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gim.2025.101402).
AlphaMissense scores each protein without its partner, and within its bands the
interface adds at most a modest signal: the intervals for pathogenic evidence
(PP3, score ≥ {PP3_SUPPORTING}) and for benign evidence reach no difference, and
where its score is indeterminate the interface makes none.

<Html html={forestPlot(alphaMissenseRows(s))} />

## Conservation explains only part of it

UCSC's phyloP across 100 vertebrates scores every base by how much more slowly
it has changed than neutral DNA would. On its own it predicts pathogenicity:

<Html html={shareBars(conservationQuartileRows(s), 'var(--pathogenic)')} />

Share of classified variants that are pathogenic, by the phyloP of the
variant's base, in quarters of all scored variants.

Interfaces are conserved too, so being at one might be conservation under
another name. So we compared pathogenic and benign variants only within the
same phyloP quarter of each protein:

<Html html={forestPlot(conservationRows(s))} />

On the folded surface the odds ratio goes from
{odds(c.orderedSurface.unmatched).toFixed(1)}× to
**{odds(c.orderedSurface.matched).toFixed(1)}×**: conservation accounts for part
of the interface signal, not most of it. These rows count only variants with a
phyloP score, and a quarter of a protein counts only when it holds both a
pathogenic and a benign variant. The matched surface row therefore rests on
{counted(c.orderedSurface.matched).toLocaleString()} of
{counted(c.orderedSurface.unmatched).toLocaleString()} variants. Quarters are
coarse.

## {v.uncertain.toLocaleString()} variants of uncertain significance sit at an interface

Most variants at an interface have no verdict yet. Each one now has a
structural hypothesis: the partner it may disturb.

### How many reach AlphaMissense's pathogenic evidence?

<Html html={callBars(am)} />

Share of scored variants at an interface whose AlphaMissense score is at least
supporting evidence of pathogenicity in ACMG terms (PP3, ≥ {PP3_SUPPORTING}).
The uncertain ones fall between the known pathogenic and benign:
{am.uncertain.pp3.toLocaleString()} of them reach it, and
{am.uncertain.bp4.toLocaleString()} reach evidence of benignity (BP4, ≤ {BP4_SUPPORTING}).
The developers' own {ALPHAMISSENSE_LIKELY_PATHOGENIC} "likely pathogenic"
cut-off does not reach even supporting evidence, so the site does not use it.

### The strongest leads

{s.leads.variants.toLocaleString()} of the uncertain variants, in
{s.leads.genes.toLocaleString()} genes, change an interface residue where a
substitution is already classified pathogenic: the same amino-acid change for
{s.leads.sameChange} of them (ACMG PS1), a different one for the rest (PM5).
They are on the [Leads](../../leads/) page. The best of them sit on interfaces
that experiment confirms.

PSEN1 {psen1.change}, for instance, is uncertain, but
{psen1.sameResidue.join(' and ')} at the same residue is a pathogenic cause of
early-onset Alzheimer disease. AlphaMissense scores {psen1.change}{' '}
{psen1.alphaMissense.toFixed(2)}, {evidenceText(psen1.alphaMissense)} on the
calibrated scale. Residue {psen1.position} is on presenilin's predicted
interface with APH1A. The γ-secretase structure
{aph1a.experiment.pdb.toUpperCase()} puts {aph1a.experiment.experimental}
residues of presenilin against APH1A, and the prediction has
{aph1a.experiment.shared} of them.

## Caveats

Only {percent(s.withPartner, s.proteins)} of disease proteins have any predicted
partner, and the complexes are predictions: an interface variant is a lead to
test. The method is on the [About](../../about/) page.
