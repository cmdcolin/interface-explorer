We compared where ClinVar missense variants fall in disease proteins that have
a confident AlphaFold DB complex. {percent(pn, pd)} of pathogenic variants
({pn.toLocaleString()} of {pd.toLocaleString()}) sit at a predicted interface,
against {percent(bn, bd)} of benign ones ({bn.toLocaleString()} of
{bd.toLocaleString()}). Compared within each protein, that is an odds ratio of
**{odds(e.all).toFixed(1)}**.

The rows check the obvious confounders:

- **Folded regions.** Interfaces are folded, and so is the buried core where
  pathogenic variants concentrate. On folded, surface-exposed residues alone the
  odds ratio is {odds(e.orderedSurface).toFixed(1)}.
- **Conservation.** Interfaces are conserved, and conserved bases are more often
  pathogenic. Comparing variants only within the same phyloP quarter of each
  protein, the odds ratio goes from {odds(c.orderedSurface.unmatched).toFixed(1)} to
  {odds(c.orderedSurface.matched).toFixed(1)}.
- **Prediction confidence.** The odds ratio is about the same in every ipSAE band.
- **AlphaMissense.** Within its calibrated evidence bands
  ([Bergquist et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gim.2025.101402)) the
  difference is small or absent, so the interface adds little to a predictor that
  already scores these residues.

Earlier work has made the same comparison; the
[comparison](../../comparison/) page lists it.

## Variants without a classification

{v.uncertain.toLocaleString()} variants of uncertain significance sit at a
predicted interface, against {v.pathogenic.toLocaleString()} pathogenic and
{v.benign.toLocaleString()} benign. Each gene page lists them with the partner
whose interface they sit on. {s.leads.variants.toLocaleString()} of them, in
{s.leads.genes.toLocaleString()} genes, change a residue where another
substitution is classified pathogenic; the [Leads](../../leads/) page lists
those.

## Caveats

- Only {percent(s.withPartner, s.proteins)} of disease proteins have any
  confident predicted partner.
- The complexes are predictions. A variant at an interface may matter for
  folding, for another partner or not at all, so its position does not show
  that it changes the binding.
- The method is on the [About](../../about/) page.
