Splicing changes interfaces too. APP695 (P05067-4), the neuronal isoform of
APP, has no Kunitz inhibitor domain, so it lacks most of APP's predicted
interfaces with the proteases KLK11, KLK14 and prostasin; APP751 (P05067-8)
keeps the domain and lacks none.
