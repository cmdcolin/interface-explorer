Many disease proteins work as two or more copies of themselves. A variant
where two copies meet can loosen the pair, or put a faulty copy into every
pair it joins. The explorer now lists each disease protein's homodimer from
AlphaFold DB beside its complexes with other proteins, as a partner called
"itself". {p.withHomodimer.toLocaleString()} genes have one, and
for {p.onlyItself.toLocaleString()} of them it is the only confident complex,
so they are new to the explorer.

We checked the predicted copy-to-copy interfaces against experiment. Of the{' '}
{p.checked.toLocaleString()} homodimers with a PDB entry holding two copies of
the protein, {p.confirmed.toLocaleString()} recover at least half the residues
in contact there. AlphaFold DB models only two copies, so for a protein that
assembles into four, like transthyretin, it shows one of the interfaces.

## SOD1 and ALS

SOD1, the copper-zinc superoxide dismutase, is a homodimer, and its variants
cause familial ALS. Variants that loosen the dimer are thought to free
monomers that misfold and aggregate. In AlphaFold DB's predicted SOD1

## TTR and amyloidosis

Transthyretin carries thyroxine and retinol in the blood as a tetramer, two
dimers back to back. Variants that weaken it let the tetramer come apart, and
the freed monomers misfold into the amyloid of hereditary transthyretin
amyloidosis. Tafamidis treats it by binding the tetramer and holding it
together. In AlphaFold DB's TTR homodimer (ipSAE {ttr.ipsae}),{' '}
{ttr.residues} residues of each copy touch the other, and PDB{' '}
{ttr.experiment?.pdb.toUpperCase()} confirms {ttr.experiment?.shared} of its{' '}
{ttr.experiment?.experimental} contacts.

## Caveats

AlphaFold DB models two copies, so a tetramer or a larger ring shows one of its
interfaces, and the PDB check compares the model with whatever copies one
crystal holds. An interface variant in a recessive enzyme, such as PAH or GALT,
more likely weakens the complex than poisons it. Telling those apart needs each
variant's inheritance, which the explorer does not yet use. The method is on
the [About](../../about/) page, and every homodimer is on its gene's page as the
partner "itself".
