BioHackathon Europe 2026 Project 9 picked {p.complexes} cryo-EM and X-ray
structures of biogenic amine receptors bound to a G protein; we added one
olfactory receptor, OR51E2. We took the residues within 5 Å across each
receptor–Gα interface and placed the ClinVar missense variants on them.

**{p.gAlphaPathogenic} pathogenic variants land on these interfaces, and all are
on the G protein: {p.gnasPathogenic} on Gαs, {p.tipPathogenic.length} of them in
its last {p.tip} residues**, the tip of the α5 helix that Gαs pushes into the
receptor's cytoplasmic cavity. The receptor side has
{p.receptorPathogenic || 'none'}.

## The receptor side

The Project 9 receptors' contact residues carry {p.uncertain} variants of
uncertain significance and {p.benign} benign ones, mostly from gene-panel
testing. That says as much about ClinVar as about the interface: GNAS is a
Mendelian disease gene (pseudohypoparathyroidism), and most amine receptors are
not. Several GPCRdb positions are hit in more than one receptor, which is what{' '}
{p.variants} variants spread over {p.positions} numbered positions would give
anyway, so we have not ranked them as leads.

## Gαolf and the olfactory receptors

Olfactory receptors signal through Gαolf (GNAL). AlphaFold DB predicts GNAL
complexes with {p.olfactory.length} olfactory receptors
({p.olfactory.join(', ')}), at ipSAE {p.ipsaeRange.join('–')}, just above the
0.6 threshold.

OR51E2 bound to propionate with mini-Gs
(<a href={`https://www.rcsb.org/structure/${p.or51e2.pdb}`}>{p.or51e2.pdb}</a>,
cryo-EM, {p.or51e2.resolution} Å; Billesbølle et al., Nature 2023) tests the
receptor side of those predictions: AlphaFold DB's
OR51E2–{p.or51e2.partner} complex covers {p.or51e2.recall}% of the{' '}
{p.or51e2.residues} receptor residues within 5 Å of Gα, and {p.or51e2.precision}%
of the residues it predicts are among them. The mini-Gs is an engineered Gαs, so
this does not test the Gαolf residues.

On Gαolf's side, ClinVar has {p.gnalVariants.length} variants of uncertain
significance; L380R, at the tip of α5 like the Gαs variants above, is the one to
look at first. GNAL is a disease gene because of dystonia, not smell, and V204E
and L380R also contact DRD5, ADRB1 and the TAARs, so this is the face Gαolf
turns to every receptor. See [GNAL's page](../../gene/GNAL/).
