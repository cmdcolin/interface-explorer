BioHackathon Europe 2026 Project 9 picked {p.complexes} cryo-EM and X-ray
structures of biogenic amine receptors bound to a G protein; we added one
olfactory receptor, OR51E2. We took the residues within 5 Å across each
receptor–Gα interface and placed the ClinVar missense variants on them.

**{p.gAlphaPathogenic} pathogenic variants land on these interfaces, and all are
on the G protein: {p.gnasPathogenic} on Gαs, {p.tipPathogenic.length} of them in
its last {p.tip} residues**, the tip of the α5 helix that Gαs pushes into the
receptor's cytoplasmic cavity. The receptor side has
{p.receptorPathogenic || 'none'}.

## The receptor side

The Project 9 receptors' contact residues carry {p.uncertain} variants of
uncertain significance and {p.benign} benign ones, mostly from gene-panel
testing. That says as much about ClinVar as about the interface: GNAS is a
Mendelian disease gene (pseudohypoparathyroidism), and most amine receptors are
not. Several GPCRdb positions are hit in more than one receptor, which is what{' '}
{p.variants} variants spread over {p.positions} numbered positions would give
anyway, so we have not ranked them as leads.

## Gαolf and the olfactory receptors

Olfactory receptors signal through Gαolf (GNAL). AlphaFold DB predicts GNAL
complexes with {p.olfactory.length} olfactory receptors

### The Gαolf side

The mini-Gs is an engineered Gαs, not Gαolf, but the two are 80% identical and
their α5 helices end alike (QYELL). Mapped onto Gαolf by sequence, the{' '}
{p.or51e2.golf.experimental} mini-Gs residues within 5 Å of OR51E2 test the other
half of the prediction. AlphaFold DB's OR51E2–Gαolf complex puts{' '}
{p.or51e2.golf.predicted} Gαolf residues against the receptor, and{' '}
{p.or51e2.golf.shared} of them are among those {p.or51e2.golf.experimental}: it
finds {p.or51e2.golf.recall}% of the experimental surface, and{' '}
{p.or51e2.golf.precision}% of what it predicts is on it. It misses only residue{' '}
{p.or51e2.golf.missed.join(', ')}.

ClinVar has {p.gnalVariants.length} variants of uncertain significance where
AlphaFold DB puts Gαolf against an olfactory receptor. Three GNAL variants are
worth placing:

- **L380R**, at the tip of α5 like the Gαs variants above: L380 (Gαs L393) is{' '}
  {p.golfReach[380]} Å from OR51E2 in {p.or51e2.pdb}, so it touches the receptor
  in the structure and in the prediction. It is the one to look at first.
- **K376Q:** K376 is the residue the prediction misses. Its Gαs counterpart,
  R389, is {p.golfReach[376]} Å from OR51E2 in {p.or51e2.pdb}; AlphaFold DB puts
  K376 against OR2A1 and OR2J3 instead.
- **D227N**, the dystonia 25 variant, is not a receptor contact. Its Gαs
  counterpart, D240, is {p.golfReach[227]} Å from OR51E2 in {p.or51e2.pdb} and{' '}
  {p.drd5Golf.reach[227]} Å from DRD5 in {p.drd5Golf.pdb}. AlphaFold DB has it
  against Gβ and the adenylyl cyclases ADCY2, ADCY3 and ADCY4, and within 5 Å
  of DRD5 in its DRD5–Gαolf complex, which {p.drd5Golf.pdb} does not bear out.

GNAL's other predicted GPCR complexes use the same face. The other olfactory
receptors ({p.or51e2.golf.olfactory.partners.join(', ')}) cover{' '}
{p.or51e2.golf.olfactory.recall} of the 8F76 surface, with{' '}
{p.or51e2.golf.olfactory.precision} of their predicted residues on it. The{' '}
{p.or51e2.golf.others.partners.length} other GPCRs, DRD5 and the trace amine
receptors among them, cover {p.or51e2.golf.others.recall} but predict larger
interfaces, only {p.or51e2.golf.others.precision} of which is on it. DRD5's own
Gs structure ({p.drd5Golf.pdb}) agrees: AlphaFold DB's DRD5–Gαolf complex covers{' '}
{p.drd5Golf.recall}% of its Gαs contacts mapped onto Gαolf, and{' '}
{p.drd5Golf.precision}% of the {p.drd5Golf.predicted} residues it predicts are
among them.

So on OR51E2, the Gαolf face AlphaFold DB predicts is the one a Gs mimic uses,
despite an ipSAE just over the threshold. A Gαs structure cannot test the fifth
of Gαolf that differs from Gαs. GNAL is a disease gene because of dystonia, not
smell; see [GNAL's page](../../gene/GNAL/).
