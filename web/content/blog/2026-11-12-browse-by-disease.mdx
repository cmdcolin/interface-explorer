The explorer shows which predicted interface a variant sits on. The disease
pages add one more fact to browse by: whether the partner on the other side is
also listed as causing the same disease.

## A page per disease

The new <a href={href('diseases/')}>Diseases</a> section has{' '}
{p.diseases.toLocaleString()} pages. Each is a disease listed on a ClinVar
missense variant classified pathogenic at a confident predicted interface. It
lists the genes with variants for that disease, the variants themselves, and
the predicted pairs whose two proteins both cause it.

Diseases are matched through [MONDO](https://mondo.monarchinitiative.org/),
which links ClinVar's conditions to the OMIM numbers UniProt gives each
gene's diseases. Many diseases come in OMIM phenotypic series, the same
phenotype caused by any of several genes, such as the subtypes of Noonan
syndrome or Fanconi anemia. {p.series.toLocaleString()} of the pages are
series.

{p.withPairs.toLocaleString()} diseases have at least one pair of predicted
partners that both cause it, {p.pairs.toLocaleString()} pairs in all, and{' '}
{p.seriesPairs.toLocaleString()} of those pairs are on series pages: the two
proteins of a complex usually cause different numbered subtypes of a disease,
not the same one.

## On gene pages

Each gene page now marks, for each variant at an interface, whether the
residue is at that gene's only interface or one shared by several partners,
and which partner causes the same disease or one in its series. A filter shows
only those variants, and the gene's disease pages are linked at the top.
Leads, the uncertain variants at a residue where another change is
pathogenic, now prefer a partner with such a disease link when they touch
several.

## What to keep in mind

- **A series can be broad.** The series with pairs range from{' '}
  {p.smallestSeries} to {p.largestSeries} diseases. A small series like
  mismatch repair cancer syndrome is specific; a large one, such as
  immunodeficiency or retinitis pigmentosa, shares only a phenotype. Each link shows the series' size.
- **ClinVar lists conditions per variant, not per classification.** A variant
  classified pathogenic for one condition may list others from different
  submissions, so a listed condition is not always the one it was classified
  for.
- **Only diseases UniProt says a gene causes count.** Susceptibility and "may
  be caused by" annotations are left out.
- **The interfaces are predictions,** and a variant at one may still act
  another way, by unfolding the protein or keeping it switched on.
